FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is everything taught in Tamil?

Yes. Concepts are explained in Tamil so they're easy to understand. Code, commands and technical terms stay in English, exactly as you'll use them at work.

Do I need prior experience?

No. Courses start from the fundamentals and build up one idea at a time. If you're new, start with Git and Python, then move to databases and backend.

Are the courses really free?

Several self-paced courses — PostgreSQL, Docker, Git and Locust — are free forever. Sign in with Google and start right away. The live cohort programs are paid, at an affordable price.

When are the live classes? What if I miss one?

Classes run in the evening (IST) on weekdays, and on weekends for AWS, so they fit around work or college. Every session is recorded and shared the same day, and you keep lifetime access.

Which program should I pick?

Want to build with LLMs? Pick AI Agent Engineering. Aiming for cloud roles or the SAA-C03 certification? Pick AWS. Into DevOps and platforms? Pick Docker & Kubernetes. Working with data? Pick Data Engineering. Not sure? Mail us at learnwithjafer@gmail.com and we'll help you choose.

How do I track my progress?

Once you sign in, every course has progress tracking and quizzes, so you always know what's done and what's next.

Will this help me get a job?

The courses focus on practical skills used in real companies — backend, cloud, DevOps and AI. There are no placement guarantees; the skills and the hands-on work are what make the difference.

How do I contact you?

For any queries — courses, enrollment or payments — send a mail to learnwithjafer@gmail.com. We'll get back to you.
